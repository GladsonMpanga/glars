HOW TO GET STARTED

A clear path
to joining us.

The steps below are a general guide. Please confirm current dates, requirements and fees directly with the school.

01

Contact the school

Ask about available places, class levels, school visits and the current application period.

02

Prepare documents

Request the official application form and the list of required learner records and supporting documents.

03

Submit your application

Follow the school's instructions for submitting forms and arranging any required assessment or meeting.

04

Receive confirmation

The school will explain the next steps, reporting date, uniform guidance and payment arrangements.

These sample FAQs can be edited once the school confirms its admission policies.

Which classes can I apply for?

Contact the school to confirm the class levels currently accepting applications and whether places are available.

What documents are required?

Ask for the current admissions checklist. Requirements may include previous school records and learner identification details.

How do I find out about fees?

Request the official fee schedule from the school. Do not rely on unofficial or outdated fee information.
